## 2. Definitions

- **Student Data**: any information about an identified or identifiable student that an Authorized User enters into, or imports into, the Service while using it for the School. It includes "pupil records" (Ed. Code §49073.1(d)), "education records" and personally identifiable information (34 C.F.R. §99.3), and "covered information" under Bus. & Prof. Code §22584 [lawyer: cite the definitions subsection]. Exhibit A lists every element.
- **Staff Data**: information about the School's employees: Authorized Users' account details and the School's staff list.
- **Member Teacher**: an Authorized User who has joined the School's TeachForFree school with the School's join link.
- **School Classes**: the classes the Service marks as the School's: those a Member Teacher creates while a member (unless they mark one as their own when creating it), and those a Member Teacher adds to the School. A teacher can't take a class out of the School; only a School Administrator can give one back to its teacher, after which it is no longer a School Class.
- **School Data**: Student Data in School Classes (with the marks, notes, groups, and conferences that belong to them), and Staff Data. A teacher's lesson plans and templates are the teacher's own work, not School Data. Student information typed into them is covered by Section 9.6.
- **Authorized User**: a School employee or contractor the School allows to use the Service for the School, signing in with their own Google account. It also includes a co-teacher given access by an Authorized User, and a substitute holding a one-day link from an Authorized User.
- **School Administrator**: an Authorized User with the administrator role in the School's TeachForFree school.
- **Subprocessor**: a third party that processes School Data for Provider to run the Service (Section 11).
- **Data Breach**: unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, or disclosure of, School Data, or a reasonable belief that one has happened.
- **Deidentified Data**: data from which all personally identifiable information has been removed so that no student can reasonably be identified.

Teachers may also use the Service on their own, outside this Agreement, for classes that aren't School Classes. Those classes, and the Student Data in them, are not School Data. They're covered by Provider's Terms and Privacy Policy, and School Administrators can't see them.

## 3. Ownership and FERPA school-official status

**3.1 School owns its data.** All School Data is and remains the property of, and under the control of, the School. Provider gets no ownership or license to it beyond what it needs to provide the Service under this Agreement. _(Ed. Code §49073.1(b)(1))_

**3.2 School official.** For Student Data, Provider is a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under 34 C.F.R. §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). Provider performs a service the School would otherwise do with its own staff. With respect to the use and maintenance of Student Data, Provider is under the School's direct control. Provider uses Student Data only for the purposes of this Agreement, and re-discloses it only as 34 C.F.R. §99.33(a) allows. The School is responsible for including vendors like Provider in its annual FERPA notice of school-official criteria (34 C.F.R. §99.7(a)(3)(iii)).

**3.3 When a Member Teacher leaves.** A School Administrator removes a departing Member Teacher in the Service (or the teacher leaves). From that moment, the teacher's School Classes leave the teacher's account: the teacher, and anyone they share with, can no longer see or change them. School Administrators can still view them, and print their reports, for [30] days. Then the Service deletes them automatically, with the students who are in none of the teacher's other classes. A School Administrator can also delete them sooner. The teacher keeps only classes that aren't School Classes. The School removes departing staff within [5] business days of their last day. Provider's duties for a departing teacher start when the School removes them in the Service or tells Provider in writing. [Lawyer: confirm that 30 days is long enough for the School's records needs. The School's official records stay in its student information system.]

**3.4 Pupil-generated content.** Students don't sign in to the Service or create content in it, so there's no pupil-generated content to transfer. _(Ed. Code §49073.1(b)(2))_ If that changes, the parties will amend this Agreement before students use any such feature.

## 4. Permitted and prohibited uses

**4.1 Permitted use.** Provider uses School Data only to provide, secure, support, and fix the Service for the School, as directed by the School and its Authorized Users. _(Ed. Code §49073.1(b)(3); Bus. & Prof. Code §22584(b)(2), (b)(4))_

**4.2 Prohibited uses.** Provider will not:

1. use School Data for any purpose not required or specifically permitted by this Agreement _(Ed. Code §49073.1(b)(3))_;
2. use Student Data, or anything learned from it, to target advertising to students, parents, or staff, on the Service or anywhere else _(Ed. Code §49073.1(b)(9); Bus. & Prof. Code §22584(b)(1))_;
3. build a profile of a student for any purpose other than the School's purposes in using the Service. Behavior marks and charts are shown only to the School's Authorized Users _(Bus. & Prof. Code §22584(b)(2))_;
4. sell, rent, or trade School Data _(Bus. & Prof. Code §22584(b)(3))_;
5. disclose School Data except to Subprocessors under Section 11, to the people an Authorized User chooses to share with (Section 7.3), as the law requires (Section 4.4), or as the School directs in writing _(Bus. & Prof. Code §22584(b)(4))_;
6. use School Data, including Deidentified Data, to train, test, or improve artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models;
7. use Student Data for marketing, or to develop products other than the Service.

**4.3 Deidentified Data.** Provider does not create or use Deidentified Data sets from Student Data. Provider may keep operational metrics that contain no Student Data, such as request counts and error rates.

**4.4 Legal demands.** If Provider receives a subpoena, court order, or other legal demand for School Data, Provider will, where the law allows, tell the School promptly and before responding, so the School can respond or object.

**4.5 Change of control.** If Provider's business is sold or merged, the successor must be bound by this Agreement for School Data already held. Otherwise, Provider will give the School [30] days' notice so it can retrieve and delete its data first.

**7.2 People.**

- **Responsible person.** [Anthony Barragan] is responsible for the security and confidentiality of School Data _(34 C.F.R. §300.623(a))_.
- **Training.** Everyone at Provider with access to School Data completes training on FERPA, IDEA confidentiality, California student privacy law, and this Agreement before getting access, and every year after _(34 C.F.R. §300.623(c))_. Training was completed on [date].
- **Access list.** Provider keeps a current list of the names and positions of everyone at Provider who can access School Data, and gives it to the School on request _(cf. 34 C.F.R. §300.623(d))_. Today: [Anthony Barragan, owner and sole developer].
- **Access records.** The Service keeps a record of access to Student Data _(34 C.F.R. §300.614; cf. §99.32)_: who (the teacher, a co-teacher, a School Administrator, a substitute's link, a family's conference code, or Provider), whose records, what they did (opened a class or a student's page, printed reports, gave or changed a mark or note, changed a roster, and so on), which class and student, and when (each person's first and last time each day). It holds IDs only, never names or text, and is kept for one year. The teacher and the School's Administrators can see any student's record of access on the student's page in the Service; Provider will export a fuller record on request within [5] business days. Provider's own access through its administrative tool is recorded the same way. Direct access to the database by Provider, outside the Service, isn't recorded by the Service.

**7.3 Who can see Student Data inside the Service.** The School acknowledges how the Service shares data among its users:

1. a teacher sees their own classes, except School Classes of a school they've left (Section 3.3);
2. a **co-teacher** the teacher invites sees, and if allowed changes, the features the teacher shares: classes (students, marks, notes), lesson plans, or conferences. A co-teacher sees the teacher's School Classes only if they are a member of the same School; a co-teacher from outside the School sees only the teacher's own classes;
3. a **School Administrator** can view, but not change, the School Classes of each Member Teacher, with their students, marks, and notes on marks, and the School Classes held after a teacher left (Section 3.3). They never see a teacher's own classes, lesson plans, or conferences. Marks on a student's page and reports are limited the same way: a student who is in both a School Class and a teacher's own class shows only the School Class's marks to the School;
4. a **substitute** with a one-day link sees that day's lesson plan and the rosters of the classes the teacher picked, and can give marks, on that day only;
5. a **family** with a conference code sees only their own student's first name and conference time;
6. **every teacher in the School** sees the School calendar's events (title, time, location, notes, guests), except private events, which only their guests, administrators, and calendar editors see.

The School is responsible for setting policy for its Authorized Users on these choices, including not putting diagnoses or disability information in notes, plans, or event titles.

**7.4 Data location.** School Data is stored and processed in the United States, at Provider's host [Hetzner, location: Ashburn, VA / Hillsboro, OR]. Subprocessors may process it in transit as described in Section 11.

### A.3 Where student names appear unencrypted

## Exhibit B: Security controls as implemented

Each control is marked **Built**: present in the code as of 2026-11-14, see `docs/compliance/audit.md` for file and line. Or **[Confirm]**: an operational setting Provider reports that can't be seen in the code, which must be confirmed before signing.

### B.1 Encryption

- **Built.** Student names, notes on marks and conferences, lesson plan text and templates, group names, school calendar event text and week messages, and queued emails are encrypted field by field with AES-256-GCM before they're written. Each value gets a fresh random nonce, and each ciphertext is bound to the record and field it belongs to, so it can't be moved to another row. Each kind of data uses its own key, derived with HKDF-SHA256 from one master key.
- **Built.** Google student IDs are stored only as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) mixed with the teacher's ID, so the same child under two teachers can't be linked. Student email addresses are never requested or stored.
- **Built.** Google Classroom access tokens are encrypted with a separate key. Session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, rotate on every use, and revoke their whole session if one is reused. Substitute, conference, co-teacher, and join-link secrets are looked up by hash, and their encrypted copies are erased once the link can't be used.
- **Built.** The master keys are read only from the server's environment. They're never in the code, the repository, the container image, or the database.
- **[Confirm]** Only [Anthony Barragan] can read the server's environment and the hosting console. Those accounts use multi-factor authentication [confirm]. The master key is backed up offline, separately from any database backup [confirm where].
- **[Confirm]** Server disk encryption: [yes / no]. Field-level encryption protects names and text either way. Marks, times, and IDs are not field-encrypted.
- Limit: the server holds the keys so it can show data to Authorized Users. Encryption protects against a copied database or backup, not against someone in control of the running server.

### B.2 Transport

- **[Confirm]** All traffic uses HTTPS through Cloudflare. HTTP redirects to HTTPS. [HSTS: confirm the Cloudflare setting.]
- **Built.** Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax in production. State-changing requests from other origins are rejected. Every API response is sent with `Cache-Control: no-store`.

### B.3 Access control

- **Built.** Sign-in is through Google only. Each Authorized User has their own account. Multi-factor authentication is whatever the School's Google Workspace requires. [The School should require 2-Step Verification for staff.] [Optional: Provider can limit sign-in to the School's Google domain, but the setting applies to the whole Service.]
- **Built.** Access tokens last 15 minutes. Sessions end after 30 days without use, and after 90 days at most.
- **Built.** Each teacher's data is separated in the database: composite foreign keys make Postgres itself refuse to mix one teacher's students, classes, or marks with another's.
- **Built.** Roles: teacher; co-teacher with view or edit per feature; School Administrator (view-only access to the School Classes of member teachers, and of teachers who left until those classes are deleted, never a teacher's own classes); calendar editor; and Provider's site administrator, who can look up an account's name and email and nothing more in the app. Every route has an explicit rule for co-teacher and administrator access, enforced and covered by tests.
- **Built.** Which classes a person can see is one rule in the database, applied to every list of classes, students, and marks, and checked for every class, student, or mark a request names; a class out of sight answers exactly like one that doesn't exist. Classes held after a teacher leaves are read-only for everyone. Leaving a School holds its classes however the membership ends, in the database itself. Covered by tests.
- **Built.** Substitute links work for one day only. Conference codes work for one student only. Co-teacher invites expire in 7 days, and school join links in 7, 30, or 90 days.
- **[Confirm]** The database is reachable only from the API server. The host firewall allows only [Cloudflare and SSH from …]. SSH uses keys only.
- **[Confirm] / Not built.** The database account the server uses can [today: change the schema, because the server runs migrations at startup (audit F7); proposed: a separate migration role].
- **Built.** Ending access at once. Every request checks that the account is still on and its session not ended, so it takes effect within one request, not when a 15-minute access token expires. Each user can sign out everywhere from their Account page, which ends every session and removes every device's push reminders. Provider can turn an account off, or end its sessions, with a command on the server; a turned-off account can't sign in, and its substitute links, family conference codes, and co-teachers' access stop. The School's view of its School Classes is unaffected.

### B.4 Abuse protection
