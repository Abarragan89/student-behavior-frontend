## Substitute links

A substitute link lets anyone who has it, on the chosen day only:

- open that day's lesson plan and check lines off
- see the names of the students in the classes you picked, and that day's mark counts
- give marks in those classes

So please:

- **Share a link only with the substitute covering that day,** and send it in a way your school allows.
- **Treat it like a key.** You're responsible for who has your link.
- **Turn it off if it goes to the wrong person,** or if plans change. It stops working right away.

Substitutes who use a link also agree to these terms, including the acceptable use rules below. They may use what they see only to teach the class that day.
